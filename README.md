## About

I teach computer science and build software. Most of my work sits where those
two overlap — turning ideas into working systems, and turning working systems
into something a student can actually understand.

I care about code that is clear before it is clever, and about explanations that
respect the person reading them.

## Teaching

Teaching keeps me honest. It is difficult to explain a concept you only half
understand, so preparing to teach something is the fastest way I know to find
the gaps in my own thinking.

I try to teach the reasoning rather than the recipe — why a data structure is
shaped the way it is, not just how to use it.
